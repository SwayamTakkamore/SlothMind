# Welcome to SlothMind

SlothMind is a local-first, privacy-focused Markdown knowledge workspace.

## What you can do here
- Open or create a vault of plain `.md` files
- Edit notes with autosave and a split/source/preview-friendly workflow
- Browse with a nested file explorer
- Search across titles and content with `Ctrl+K` / `Ctrl+P`
- Keep everything on your machine with safe filesystem boundaries

## Tips
1. Create notes per project or per topic
2. Use headings and lists to make search results more useful
3. Notes are just files—back them up however you like

*Your notes, your AI, your machine.*
